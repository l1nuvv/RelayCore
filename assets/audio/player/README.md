# Player sounds (0.2)

Walking sounds are cut from the supplied `concrete_footsteps_short_3.5s.wav`, `metal_footsteps_short_3.6s.wav` and `tile_footsteps_short_3.5s.wav` recordings by `python scripts/build-player-footsteps.py`. Four isolated footfalls are selected from each source. The clips remain mono PCM16 at 44.1 kHz and receive short edge fades so they do not click when triggered.

The runtime preloads these short samples. Step timing follows grounded movement with a human cadence: about two footfalls per second while walking and three while sprinting. Blocked movement and flight do not produce footsteps. The landing and flashlight-switch sounds are generated by `python scripts/build-player-audio.py` and remain separate from the supplied walking recordings.
