# RelayCore visual direction

## Target

GoldSource/Half-Life-era spatial readability and industrial atmosphere, implemented as an original modern project rather than a copy of specific shipped assets.

Favor:

- utilitarian industrial and laboratory/facility surfaces;
- believable functional segmentation: panels, access points, vents, conduits, structural framing;
- restrained color accents used for navigation, hazard, faction/function, or interaction readability;
- clear large/medium forms before small detail;
- wear that follows use: floor traffic, corners, handles, lower wall areas, machinery contact points;
- material identity that remains readable at gameplay distance;
- slightly stylized/retro texture density when it supports coherence.

Avoid the common generated-art failure mode:

- neon cyan/magenta everywhere;
- dense random greebles on every surface;
- arbitrary hexagons/circuit traces;
- repeated warning stripes without functional reason;
- illegible pseudo-text and fake technical labels;
- uniform scratches and edge wear across the whole image;
- over-sharpened micro-noise and excessive grunge;
- glossy “AAA sci-fi” treatment on every material;
- perfectly centered/symmetrical decoration that makes spaces feel procedurally stamped;
- detail that destroys silhouette, navigation cues, or interactable readability.

## Composition heuristic

At gameplay distance the asset should first communicate:

1. what the surface/object is;
2. what function or interaction matters;
3. what area/space it belongs to;
4. only then small decorative detail.

If the fourth layer is stronger than the first three, simplify it.
