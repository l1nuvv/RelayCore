# VK14-09 — visual quality II

Scope/status belongs to the [renderer plan](../RENDERER_MODERNIZATION_PLAN.md).
The owner selected the full milestone on 2026-10-13.

## Selected contract

09a: opt-in low/high screen-space diffuse hemisphere rays, current-frame source
radiance without screen-space feedback, geometry/eligibility-rejected temporal
denoising and replacement of the covered fraction of ambient/SH diffuse.
Uncovered rays retain authored/SH lighting. Material/screen AO is applied once
to indirect lighting; neither direct light nor emission is occluded twice.

09b: opt-in low/high roughness-aware reflection cone sampling with bounded
depth traversal/refinement, hit confidence and existing probe fallback.
Legacy SSR remains available. Deterministic rays avoid stochastic shimmer;
optional existing TAA filters the final reflection composition.

09c/d: bounded depth-of-field and previous-minus-current velocity motion blur
after temporal resolve, before bloom/tone mapping. Reject unknown motion,
transparent/overlay/weapon inputs and incompatible depth/normal neighbors.
HUD/menus remain native and are appended afterwards. All effects default off.

Fixtures: analytic emissive corner/rough reflector/focus planes and moving
geometry, then containment workshop with animated shotgun. Compare off/low/high,
cuts, disocclusion, exposure, resize, scale, shader reload and AO composition.
Initial local budgets at 1920x1080 RTX 4060 Ti: added SSGI pass GPU p99 <3 ms,
lens pass <1.5 ms, full combined scene <16.67 ms. Record reflection source/world
cost and memory separately. Off parity, validation-clean executed checks and
motion captures are required; human art review remains independent.

## Execution

The initial native-resolution, globally aligned ray pattern produced visible
streaks around the emissive analytic fixture. An isolated moving-camera/animated
shotgun 1080p capture also exceeded the selected budget: SSGI low/high GPU p99
4.436/6.670 ms, combined high scene p99 19.121 ms; high DoF lens p99 2.546 ms.
Retained evidence: `build/vk14-09/profile-before-filter/` (Tracy, CPU/GPU CSV,
summary, log and stills), `build/vk14-09/initial-review.png` and initial motion BMPs.
This prompted half-resolution GI with deterministic per-pixel ray rotation,
bilateral reconstruction and matching history-footprint rejection; lens sampling
was bounded to four/eight taps. The first half-resolution run retained eight
high-tier rays and measured 3.252 ms SSGI p99 against the 3 ms pass budget,
despite combined high meeting its frame budget. High SSGI now uses six rays.
The next capture met every GPU budget but overlapped a short CPU compilation;
the final clean repeat below is the accepted timing evidence.

## Quality review and remaining acceptance

Inspected analytic off/low/high and raw GI contact sheets, matched lighting-change
history/fresh frames, real workshop motion contact sheets and the scrollable RML
settings capture. Retained GIFs/contact sheets are `build/vk14-09/gi-motion.gif`,
`g019-motion.gif`, `gi-motion-contact.png`, `g019-motion-contact.png`,
`gi-lighting-change.png`, `final-review.png` and `g019-final.png`;
source BMPs are in `build/msvc-debug/tests/vulkan-mesh-captures/`.
The initial directional GI streaks are gone. GI is intentionally subtle on the
workshop's baked lighting; reflections spread across rough surfaces; bounded
lens kernels soften scene detail while preserving the weapon and native UI.
The raw GI diagnostic still exposes sparse sampling; filtering and optional TAA
do not make screen-space lighting complete or physically exact.

These are technical checks and agent image inspection. Owner art preference and
full human G-019 playthrough remain separate acceptance activities. Other GPUs,
physical HDR monitor appearance, hardware ray tracing and hidden/off-screen
lighting are not covered. Small DoF kernels preserve silhouettes and intentionally
do not produce large cinematic foreground bokeh. Unknown/transparent/overlay
motion stays sharp; screen-space coverage changes can remain visible.

Implementation ownership: `engine_config` validates authored controls;
`vulkan_mesh_renderer` owns optional targets, publication, history and ordering;
`advanced.slang`/`scene_effects.slang` implement screen/lens sampling;
`mesh.slang` owns indirect-light composition. Game settings/menu/reload preserve
qualities. CMake registers offline shader compilation, and config/settings/UI/
mesh fixtures protect the behavior. Runtime controls/composition remain solely
in [RENDERING](../RENDERING.md#advanced-lighting-and-lens-effects--vk14-09).
Changed C++ passes `clang-format --dry-run --Werror`; local documentation links
and `git diff --check` pass. The renderer-plan status records all four tasks as
implemented with local checks/budgets passed and independent human/hardware
acceptance still explicit.
