## 07a — frame graph and source specialization

The actual High frame order in `State::render` is:

1. Shadow maps: depth-only directional/spot maps and point-face array. Disabled
   layers are cleared; enabled layers also receive attachment clears and draws.
2. Full-resolution, single-sample pass inputs: RGBA32F normal/depth and RGBA32F
   velocity/correspondence, plus private depth. Optional SSAO is RGBA32F.
3. SSR/SSGI source: clear the current FP16 source history to black; opaque world
   shading at the scene's MSAA4 setting, resolve, then copy to source history.
   The source excludes screen reflections, screen diffuse and camera-to-hit fog,
   while retaining authored material/probe/direct light/emission and AO semantics.
4. Half-resolution FP16 diffuse GI tracing/history (ceil(width/2), ceil(height/2)).
5. World opaque/overlay/transparent rendering into FP16 MSAA4, resolve; viewmodel
   loads the resulting scene color with its existing depth policy.
6. Optional diagnostic history copy, TAA, optional lens, bloom, tone mapping,
   RmlUi and display/presentation copy. UI remains after scene color conversion.

The existing two slots, shared graphics timeline, barriers, current/previous
history descriptors and reload/reset ownership remain authoritative. This is a
source audit, not a hardware bandwidth/occupancy measurement.

Candidate A: specialize the existing source-pass state with SPIR-V constant 0.
Two source pipelines (indexed and optional mesh) share the same shader modules,
descriptor layout and geometry. Stage them transactionally on reload and retire
after the existing wait. No extra image, pass or history is introduced. Source
flags remain for eligibility and parity; the constant enables driver elimination
of reflection/GI/fog branches. Pipeline creation/memory may increase; stop if
source/scene gains do not exceed repeat variation, image parity fails, or tails
regress. No occupancy/register-count claim is made without driver evidence.

intensity is zero, and spotlight BRDF even when the spotlight is disabled.

unchanged static/motion images and world/source/scene gains beyond variation;
otherwise restore the original shader. The older uncommitted probe-fallback

## 07f — AO bandwidth and existing submission paths

Candidate F stores AO as R32_SFLOAT instead of RGBA32F: `ao_fragment` writes
identical components and every consumer reads `.r`. The precision and scalar
calculation stay float32. Select this format once for an enabled AO backend
when sampled/color-attachment usage is supported; otherwise preserve RGBA32F.
The two slot owners, dimensions, descriptors and barriers do not change. No
normal/depth/velocity packing, MSAA reduction, sorting or geometry deletion is
part of this candidate. Payload savings are deterministic; actual VMA usage and
timing must be measured independently rather than inferred from the format.

The existing matrix is ordinary mesh/pooled/instanced versus indexed geometry,
indexed materials, direct/indirect submission and graphics-queue GPU culling,
one factor at a time. VRS/async-compute have no scene consumers in the selected
backend and are recorded as absent rather than implemented to fill a matrix.
Defaults remain the author's mesh-enabled, pooled, instanced selection unless
an explicitly accepted change says otherwise.
